Self-hosted webfonts
====================

Every font in this directory is licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1. The full licence text is at https://openfontlicense.org and is
distributed with each family's upstream source, linked below.

The .woff2 files here are the unmodified Latin (and, for the two interface
families, Latin-Extended) subsets served by Google Fonts, retrieved on
2026-10-26 and vendored so that no page on this site contacts a third party.
The @font-face rules in fonts.css reproduce the upstream subset boundaries and
unicode-range values verbatim, so rendering is unchanged.
